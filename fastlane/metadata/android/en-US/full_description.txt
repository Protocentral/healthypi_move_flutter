HealthyPi Move is the companion app for the HealthyPi Move wearable, an open
hardware health monitor from ProtoCentral.

Everything runs on your phone. There is no account to create, no cloud service,
and no backend — your readings are stored in a local database on your device and
go nowhere else. The app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs.

Features

* Live view — watch ECG, PPG, heart rate, SpO2 and skin temperature stream from
  the watch in real time over Bluetooth LE.
* Trends — hourly and daily summaries with a rolling 30-day baseline, so a
  reading is shown against your own usual rather than a generic chart. Day,
  week, month and 6-month ranges, with a detail view per metric.
* Continuous HRV and a stress score derived from it. While the app is still
  learning your baseline it says so, rather than showing a meaningless zero.
* Recordings library — episodic ECG, PPG, GSR/EDA, HRV and motion captures are
  listed as soon as the watch reports them and downloaded only when you tap one.
  Every download is checksum-verified.
* Export any recording as CSV to analyse or share elsewhere.
* Firmware updates for the watch, over the air, from the app.
* Resumable sync — an interrupted transfer picks up where it stopped, and data
  is only acknowledged to the watch once it is written to your phone and
  verified, so a failed sync cannot lose readings.

Requirements

Version 3 of the app requires version 3 firmware on the watch; the older
transfer protocol has been removed. Use the firmware updater on the Device tab
to bring the watch to a matching release. Requires Android 5.0 (API 21) or
later, Bluetooth LE, and the HealthyPi Move hardware — the app does nothing on
its own.

Not a medical device

HealthyPi Move is intended for wellness, education and research. The readings it
shows, including the estimated blood pressure range, are not a diagnosis and
must not be used to make medical decisions. Talk to a clinician about anything
that concerns you.
